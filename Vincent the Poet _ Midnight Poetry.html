The poet behind the words

Meet Vincent
Lengerich.

Born in Chicago, Vincent is a devotee of poetry's oldest art. For more than forty years, he has taken his verse to forums, stages, and venues, bringing a distinctive voice to rhyme, meter, and what he calls “prosetry.”

This space is designed to introduce readers to the person behind the work, and to invite them into a lifetime of words.
